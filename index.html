About

I design clocking circuits for high-speed wireline transceivers at MediaTek in Hsinchu, Taiwan. My work covers TX serializer skew calibration, multi-phase PLL clock alignment, and RX clock distribution for 224-Gb/s and 320-Gb/s SerDes in TSMC 3-nm.

I received my M.S. and B.S. from National Taiwan University. My graduate research with Prof. Tsung-Hsien Lin focused on frequency synthesis, specifically low-jitter fractional output dividers with digital background calibration.
